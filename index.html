    Is your live journalism project missing?

    Tell EJC about it with the entry form on ejc.net. EJC reviews every entry before it appears on the map.

    The form asks for:

    • your project's title and a short description
    • where it takes place: city and country
    • an image or a video link, and your website
    • up to five events, each with a date and a link
    • the type of live journalism
    • your contact details, which are not published
    Open the entry form on ejc.net ↗

    Opens in a new tab.

    Prototype notes: the background maps are free, open map styles (OpenFreeMap by default), each credited in the map's corner. Spotted a mistake in a map? Report it to OpenStreetMap. Videos only load from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) when you press play. The pins are EJC's approved projects; one test entry, Live Magazine, also shows places from submissions still awaiting EJC review, labelled as such. Names, email addresses and job titles of the people who submitted are not included. The button under the map opens EJC's live entry form on ejc.net, so entries go straight to EJC.
